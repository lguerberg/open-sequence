## Does it work with my CRM?

Yes, with any of them, because it does not depend on one. People in your CRM become contacts keyed by your record id (`PUT /v1/contacts/{your id}`), your reps become senders keyed by your user id, and every webhook carries those ids back. The service writes nothing to your CRM: handle `signal.received` and write the reply or the meeting yourself. The one CRM-specific feature is optional: with an Attio key, `crm.*` conditions read the person, company and open deal at decision time. [Integrate OpenSequence into your product](/docs/integrate), [Webhook recipes](/docs/webhooks/recipes).

## Can I use my own UI and design system?

That is the only option. OpenSequence ships no screens: your product renders the sequence list, the editor, the people running, the senders and the health panel from the API, in your own components. The screens a product needs, with the endpoints each one reads and the actions it calls, are listed on the [integration page](/docs/integrate#admin-ui-you-will-build); [Building a canvas](/docs/sequences/building-a-canvas) covers the editor.

## Where do credentials live?

In the service's Postgres, encrypted under `SEQ_ENCRYPTION_KEYS`: mailbox passwords, the Allo and Attio keys, webhook signing secrets. No endpoint returns one; connections show hosts, ports and a status only. Your own secrets (the project API key, the webhook secret shown once) live in your app, in a secrets manager or an encrypted column. Back up the database and the keys variable together. [Security](/docs/security/security), [Storing the secret](/docs/webhooks/verifying#storing-the-secret).

## What happens when a contact replies?

The poll job reads the sender's mailbox over IMAP (or Allo reports the text), matches the message to the thread the service started, and records a `reply` signal. The first reply is attributed to the step that earned it and sets `repliedAt` on the enrollment. If the sequence has `reply` in `exit_on` (the default for outreach), the enrollment exits with `exit_reason: "replied"`. You receive `signal.received` with `data.contact.external_id` (your id) and then `enrollment.exited`. Out-of-office auto-replies are recorded as `ooo` and do not count. [Email via SMTP and IMAP](/docs/sending/email-smtp#replies-auto-replies-and-bounces), [Outcomes](/docs/analytics/outcomes).

## Does STOP stop everything?

A text made only of `STOP`, `QUIT`, `END`, `CANCEL`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, `OPT OUT` or `STOPALL` suppresses SMS for that contact and exits every enrollment they are in, email steps included. Email keeps going for them only if you enroll them again in a sequence without SMS steps. An unsubscribe click on the hosted link suppresses email the same way. [Consent and suppressions](/docs/contacts/consent-and-suppressions).

## Can reps send from their own mailbox?

Yes, and that is the model: every sender has their own mailbox over SMTP and IMAP and their own Allo number, so messages come from the rep, land in the rep's sent folder, and replies arrive in the rep's inbox. There is no shared sending domain to warm up. Daily caps, a minimum gap between sends and a send window apply per sender. [Senders](/docs/sending/senders), [Deliverability and compliance](/docs/sending/deliverability-and-compliance).

## Gmail without OAuth?

Yes. Gmail and Google Workspace connect with an **app password** (the account needs 2-step verification on) over `smtp.gmail.com:465` and `imap.gmail.com:993`. There is no Google OAuth flow to build or verify. Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com cannot connect at all: Microsoft retired password sign-in for them. Fastmail, iCloud and any mail server with password login work. [Gmail and Google Workspace](/docs/sending/email-smtp#gmail-and-google-workspace).

## What happens to people in a sequence when I publish a new version?

Nothing. An enrollment is pinned to the version it started on, and that version is immutable, so they finish the steps they were promised. New enrollments use the new default. To move people over, exit them and enroll them again; they restart from step one and the re-enrollment rule (`settings.reenroll`) applies. [Versions](/docs/sequences/versions).

## Can a contact be in two sequences at once?

No. One active enrollment per contact per project. Enrolling a batch that contains such a contact fails with `409 ALREADY_IN_SEQUENCE`, names the conflicts in `details`, and enrolls nobody. Exit them first or take them out of the selection. [Enrolling](/docs/contacts/enrolling).

## How do I test locally without sending anything?

Run the service with mock providers (`SEQ_DEV_MOCK_PROVIDERS`, on in the quickstart's Docker Compose file). The mock mailbox accepts any login and keeps sent mail in memory, the contact's timeline shows each message with its rendered body, and `POST /hooks/mock/{project slug}` injects a reply, a bounce or a STOP as if the provider had reported it. The same API, the same webhooks, no real mail. [Quickstart](/docs/get-started/quickstart).

## What are the rate limits?

600 requests per minute per API key (per `Authorization` header value), then `429` with a `Retry-After` header. Batches are sized so a sync rarely gets near it: 500 contacts per `contacts:batch`, 5,000 contacts per enroll call, 500 events per `contacts:events`. Lists page at up to 200 rows. [Conventions](/api/conventions#rate-limits), [Limits](/reference/limits).

## What does self-hosting cost?

One container and a Postgres you already run; nothing per seat, per contact or per message, and your mailbox provider and Allo bill you directly. [Install](/docs/self-hosting/install).

## What is the licence?

Apache-2.0, for the service, the SDKs and the MCP server. Run it, embed it in a commercial product, modify it; keep the notices.

## Can I change the sender of someone already running?

No. The sender is fixed when the enrollment starts. To move a rep's people to a colleague, exit them (`POST /v1/enrollments:exit { sequence, status: "active" }` or by contact) and enroll them again as the new sender. They restart from step one and `settings.reenroll` applies. [Lifecycle](/docs/contacts/lifecycle).

## Can an AI agent use it?

Yes, through the MCP server (`@opensequence/mcp`), hosted over HTTP or local over stdio, with a project key. Give agents an `enroll`-scoped key: they can read, upsert contacts, preview and enroll, complete tasks and report events, and nothing else. [MCP server](/sdks/mcp).
