OpenSequence is a self-hosted service that runs multi-step outreach for your application: emails, texts, waits, conditions and call tasks, sent from your own users' mailboxes and numbers. Your app talks to it over a small HTTP API and hears back through signed webhooks; the service does the scheduling, sending, reply tracking and bookkeeping.

It is for engineers who want sequences inside their own CRM or product without renting a sales engagement tool: you keep your UI, your data and your users, and run one Docker image next to a Postgres you own.

## How these docs are organised

Start with **Get started**: the quickstart runs on your laptop in about ten minutes, and the three pages after it explain the model and the machinery. **Use cases** shows complete flows. The middle sections (**Sending**, **Sequences**, **Contacts and enrollments**, **Webhooks**, **Analytics**) each cover one part of the API. **Self-hosting** and **Security** are for whoever runs the service. Every endpoint is in the [API reference](/api/conventions), and the SDKs and the CLI have their own tab.
