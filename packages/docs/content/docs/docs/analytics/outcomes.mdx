**The only two outcomes are replies and meetings booked**, attributed to a sequence, a channel and a step. There is no goal, no funnel and no score.

## Definitions

- **Reply**: an inbound email in a thread the service started, an inbound text, a reply you report, or a task completed with an outcome in its `connected_outcomes` (a `call_connected` signal). Out-of-office auto-replies, bounces and opt-outs are not replies.
- **Meeting booked**: a new event on the sender's Google Calendar with the contact as a non-declined attendee (detected by the minute poll), or a `meeting_booked` you report. Both are idempotent by their ids and counted once per enrollment, so reporting a booking the calendar already showed creates no second meeting.
- **Attribution window**: the signal is matched to enrollments whose last touch is within `settings.attribution_days` (14 by default) before it occurred.
- **Counted once per enrollment**: the **first** reply and the **first** meeting are attributed and set `replied_at` / `meeting_booked_at` on the enrollment, never rewritten; later ones are stored with `attribution: "none"` and appear only in the timeline.

## Attribution rules

| Signal | Credited run | Attribution |
|---|---|---|
| reply on channel X | the latest sent touch on channel X before the reply, in this enrollment | `direct` |
| reply on channel X, no touch on X | the latest sent touch on any channel | `assisted` |
| connected call | the task's own run | `direct` |
| meeting booked | the most recently touched enrollment of the contact in the window, even one that already exited (a reply often exits it; the meeting comes later); then the latest touch before the booking | `direct` |

The result is written once on the signal (`attributed_run`, `attribution`) and on the enrollment, and shows in the journey and in `signal.received`.

## Filters

Every analytics endpoint accepts `version` (a version id), `from` and `to` (ISO dates, on the enrollment's creation), `sender` (a sender id) and `country` (the contact's). Responses are not cached.

## What the numbers are made from

The endpoints read the ledgers directly (enrollments, runs, signals) with no pre-aggregation to keep correct, which is fine at the volumes the service targets. See [Endpoints](/docs/analytics/endpoints) for each one.
