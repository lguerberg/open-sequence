The short version: OpenSequence is an engine behind an API, not a product with a UI; it sends through your users' own accounts; it runs on your infrastructure; its only outcomes are replies and meetings. From Svix it copied the shape of a self-hosted service (one image, your database, your queue, an operator token, per-tenant keys) and the webhook delivery contract.</Note>
