## The schedule

A delivery is attempted at once, then after each failure with this backoff, about three days in total:

| Attempt | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delay before it | 0 | 5 s | 5 min | 30 min | 2 h | 5 h | 10 h | 10 h | 10 h |

Each attempt waits up to 10 seconds for your response. A `2xx` ends it (`delivered`); after the ninth failure the delivery is `failed` for good and only a recover or a replay sends it again. The delivery row records every attempt, the last status code and the last error.

## Disabled endpoints

An endpoint whose deliveries have **all** failed for three days (the oldest pending delivery is older than that) is set to `disabled`, with `disabled_reason` and `disabled_at`; its remaining deliveries are held, not lost; and `endpoint.disabled` is delivered to the project's other endpoints. An endpoint whose signing secret can no longer be decrypted (the encryption key was removed from `SEQ_ENCRYPTION_KEYS`) is disabled the same way: rotate the secret, then enable.

`POST /v1/webhooks/{id}/enable` sets it back to `active` and re-queues every held delivery, oldest first. Nothing was lost.

## Recover and replay

- `POST /v1/webhooks/{id}/recover { since }` re-sends every delivery of the endpoint that **failed for good** since that time, with a fresh attempt counter.
- `POST /v1/webhooks/deliveries/{id}/replay` re-sends one delivery whatever its state (delivered, failed, pending), with a fresh counter. The endpoint must be active (`409 ENDPOINT_DISABLED`).

A replayed delivery keeps its `webhook-id`, so an idempotent receiver ignores a duplicate.

## The deliveries log

`GET /v1/webhooks/{id}/deliveries` (paginated, `updated_since`) returns, per delivery: `id`, `event_id`, `event_type`, `enrollment`, `status` (`pending`, `delivered`, `failed`, `disabled`), `attempts`, `next_attempt_at`, `last_status_code`, `last_error`, `payload`, `created_at`, `delivered_at`. Delivered rows are pruned after 30 days.

## Ordering during failures

Deliveries for the same endpoint and enrollment go one at a time in creation order: while one is retrying, the ones behind it wait (`waiting`), and go out as soon as it succeeds or gives up. Other enrollments and other endpoints are not affected.

## What to alert on

An endpoint with `status: "disabled"`, a delivery with `attempts >= 5`, or `last_error` repeating across deliveries. The operator's `GET /v1/admin/engine/health` and the project's `GET /v1/engine/health` show the `seq_webhooks` queue depth.
