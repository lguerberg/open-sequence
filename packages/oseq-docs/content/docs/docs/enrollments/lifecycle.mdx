## Statuses

```text
                 ┌──────────── resume / paused_until ────────────┐
                 ▼                                               │
  enroll ──► active ──── pause, ooo reply, step_failed ────► paused
               │  ▲                                              
               │  └──── cap lifted ──── waiting_limit (over daily_limit)
               │
               ├──► completed   (an end node, an unwired transition, a task with no next step)
               ├──► exited      (reply or meeting per exit_on, opt-out, bounce, suppression, manual, deleted)
               └──► failed      (reserved)
```

| Status | Meaning | Counts as "in a sequence" |
|---|---|---|
| `active` | running, or waiting on a timer or an event | yes |
| `paused` | nothing happens until resumed; `pause_reason` says why (`manual`, `ooo`, `step_failed`), `paused_until` when it ends by itself | yes |
| `waiting_limit` | the next send is deferred by the sender's daily cap | yes |
| `completed` | reached the end | no |
| `exited` | ended early; `exit_reason` says why | no |
| `failed` | reserved; not produced today (a failed step pauses with `step_failed` instead) | no |

## Pause and resume

`POST /v1/enrollments/{id}/pause` (scope `enroll`) takes an optional `reason` (up to 40 characters, default `manual`) and `until`. Pausing an already paused enrollment is a no-op; pausing a finished one is `409 NOT_ACTIVE`. A step an executor is already running finishes; nothing more happens until resume. `resume` needs `paused` or `waiting_limit` (`409 NOT_PAUSED`) and makes the step that was due run on the next tick; a `paused_until` in the past resumes by itself.

The service pauses on its own in two cases: an out-of-office auto-reply (`ooo`, until the stated return date or 7 days) and a step that failed with no `failed` transition (`step_failed`, with `run.failed` and `enrollment.paused` delivered so you can look).

## Exit

`POST /v1/enrollments/{id}/exit` (scope `admin`) ends the enrollment with `exit_reason` `manual` (or the `reason` you pass, up to 40 characters) and your `label`, closes the step it was waiting on, cancels its open task, and delivers `enrollment.exited`. Exiting a finished enrollment returns it unchanged.

`POST /v1/enrollments:exit` exits by selector: `{ "contact": "p_7" }` exits the contact's active enrollment; `{ "sequence": "seq_…", "status": "active" }` exits up to 200 enrollments of the sequence in that status (call again for more). `PATCH /v1/sequences/{id}` with `status: "archived", exit_active: true` does the same for a whole sequence.

## Exit reasons

| `exit_reason` | Cause |
|---|---|
| `replied` | a reply or a connected call, with `reply` in `exit_on` |
| `meeting_booked` | a calendar booking or a reported meeting, with `meeting_booked` in `exit_on` |
| `opt_out` | STOP, the opt-out link, or a reported opt-out |
| `bounced` | a hard bounce or a complaint |
| `suppressed` | a reported suppression (do not contact, left company, …) |
| `manual` | an API exit, or a sequence archived with `exit_active` |
| `deleted` | the contact was erased |

## Re-enrollment

A contact who finished or exited a sequence can be enrolled in it again according to that version's `settings.reenroll`: `never` (default) skips them with `already_enrolled_before`, `always` allows it, `after_<n>d` allows it n days after the previous enrollment finished (`reenroll_before_<n>d` otherwise). The rule is checked at enrollment, per sequence, not per version. A contact can always be enrolled in a different sequence once the previous enrollment is no longer active.

## Disabled senders

`DELETE /v1/senders/{id}` refuses new enrollments as that sender; enrollments already running keep going on the sender's channels as long as those channels are active. To stop them, exit by selector or remove the channels.
